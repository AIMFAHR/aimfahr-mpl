# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-25

- Bundled AIMFAHR logos (mark, icon, wordmark, horizontal, tagline, stacked) in dark and light versions.
- `add_logo()`: place a logo on an axes or figure like a legend, including `loc="best"`, `bbox_to_anchor`, `"outside ..."` figure locations and dragging.
- `logo_path()` and `LOGOS`.
- `journal` context and `journal_size()` for single, one-and-a-half and double column figures.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

- `dark` and `light` themes; `web`, `slides` and `poster` contexts.
- Colour-blind-checked colour cycles, bundled Montserrat, AIMFAHR colormaps (`aimfahr_sun`, `aimfahr_sun_dark`, `aimfahr_ice`, `aimfahr_div`, `aimfahr_div_dark`).
- `use()`, `direct_labels()`, `colormaps()`.
